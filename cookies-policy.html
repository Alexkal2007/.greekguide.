Cookies Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This page explains what we use them for on GREEK-ISLAND-GUIDE.

1. Essential storage

This site stores your language preference (English or Greek) in your browser's local storage so that it can show the site in the same language the next time you visit a page. This is not a tracking cookie — it simply remembers your choice.

2. Analytics cookies

If enabled, analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are most popular, so that we can improve the guide over time. These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous usage patterns rather than information that identifies you personally.

3. Advertising

This site may display advertising to support the cost of running the guide. Advertising partners may use cookies to show relevant ads and to measure how effective they are. You can usually control ad-related cookies through your browser settings or through opt-out tools provided by advertising networks.

4. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view, delete and block cookies through their settings menu. Blocking all cookies may affect how well some parts of this site work, such as remembering your language preference.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the tools we use. Any updates will be posted on this page.
